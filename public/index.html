When do alerts happen?

The device measures how far the water is below it. A smaller number means the water is higher.

Water is safe11 centimeters or more below the device
Water is rising — slow beeps7 to under 11 centimeters below the device
Water is too high — fast beepsLess than 7 centimeters below the device
No readingThe reader cannot see the water
Power Source indicator when water is safeTurns on by itself, unless turned off after a high-water alert
Power Source indicator when water is risingKeeps its current setting
Power Source indicator when water is too highTurns off by itself
Power Source indicator when there is no readingKeeps its current setting

The reader may not work when the water is very close. Keep the device dry and check it often. Do not use it as your only flood warning.
